Core module: UAE Corporate Tax & VAT

Corporate Tax

  1. How it works: who pays, the 0% and 9% rates
  2. Registration on the FTA's EmaraTax portal
  3. Working out taxable income from the accounts
  4. Exempt income and allowable deductions
  5. Small Business Relief and Free Zone rules
  6. Transfer pricing basics
  7. Preparing and filing the CT return

VAT

  1. Basics: the 5% rate, zero-rated and exempt supplies
  2. VAT registration and thresholds
  3. Tax invoices and credit notes
  4. Recovering input VAT
  5. Reverse charge mechanism
  6. Preparing and filing VAT returns on EmaraTax
  7. VAT records and compliance
