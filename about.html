ABOUT SKALEX

From classroom theory to real work

We are a group of commerce professionals who saw how many students struggle to turn classroom theory into workplace skills. Skalex was created to close that gap.

Every class teaches real work, with real tools, based on how companies actually operate. Our goal is simple: make you employable and ready for entry-level roles in India and abroad.

How we teach

01

A simulated office

You handle day-to-day accounting tasks, reconciliations, tax filings and software workflows, the way you would in a real job.

02

CA mentorship

Practising Chartered Accountants teach classes, review your work and give you honest feedback.

03

Job-ready, not just certified

Interview preparation and an experience certificate on completing the course and its practical assignments.
